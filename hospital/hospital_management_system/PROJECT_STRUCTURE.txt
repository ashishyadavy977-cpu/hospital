DATABASE MODELS:
================

HospitalInfo: Hospital details and stats
├─ name, description, rating
├─ phone, email, address
└─ total_beds, emergency_number

Doctor:
├─ name, specialization, experience
├─ phone, email, image
├─ bio, availability

Staff:
├─ name, position, department
├─ phone, email, image

Service:
├─ name, description, charge
└─ category

Appointment:
├─ patient_name, patient_email, patient_phone
├─ doctor_id (FK), appointment_date
├─ reason, status

Bill:
├─ patient_name, patient_email
├─ service_id (FK), appointment_id (FK)
├─ amount, description, status
└─ due_date

Rating:
├─ patient_name, rating (1-5)
└─ comment
